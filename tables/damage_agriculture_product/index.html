    Table: damage_agriculture_product

    Description (EN): This table represents damages reported to agriculture products. In the database's inheritance architecture, it acts as a direct sub-type of the farm_damage table. It does not store redundant descriptive columns; instead, it uses a shared primary key to inherit all high-level attributes (such as name, location, management type, and geometry) from its parent damage record.

    Relationships

    • Inherits from (Sub-type of): damage and on turn farm_damage. The damage_agriculture_product table is a specialized extension of the damage table.
    • Affects: component (One or more damages affect a component or its subclasses)
    • Is reported by: survey (One or more damages is reported in a survey)
    • Reported in: attachment (A damage is reported in one or more attachments)
    • Results in: economic_loss

    !!! tip "Where is the rest of the data?" To find the name, geographical location (geometry, town, country), or other details of a damage to agriculture product, you must join this table with the damage record that shares the exact same id.

    Example Query

    Retrieve a list of all damages to agriculture product, including their descriptions, towns, and spatial coordinates, by joining the table with its parent damage entity:

    SELECT
    dap.*,
    d.*
FROM 
    damage_agriculture_product dap  join damage d on dap.id = d.id
